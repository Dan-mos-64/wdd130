WhiteWater Rafting

WhiteWater Rafting is a recreational activities company that operates in natural environments, such as rivers and lakes, using non-motorized boats. Our goal is to provide you with the equipment, instruction, and trained staff you need to enjoy guided trips with safety, protection, and fun.

History

WhiteWater Rafting started as an idea among a group of friends who wanted to encourage people to discover more of the natural beauty that this place and our country have to offer. For this reason, we decided to start working on our idea. We began training to learn how to organize and guide trips on rivers and lakes safely, using the necessary equipment and following proper safety procedures. After a few months, we opened WhiteWater Rafting. With three years of experience and many positive testimonials from our customers, we now help people from all over discover and enjoy the natural beauty of our country.
